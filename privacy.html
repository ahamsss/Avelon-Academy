Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to teach your child and run our classes. We never sell your information. Some of our tutors and service providers are outside Canada, and this policy explains where and why. Questions? Email contact@avelonacademy.ca.

1. Who we are

Avelon Academy ("Avelon", "we", "us") provides live online tutoring for students in Grades 1–12. We are based in Alberta, Canada. We handle personal information in line with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), as they apply to us.

2. What we collect

  • Parent or guardian details: name, email address, phone number, time zone and preferred contact method.
  • Student details: first name, grade, subjects, school curriculum or province, learning goals, and any learning needs you choose to tell us about.
  • Class information: class schedule, attendance, class length, short progress notes written by tutors, homework, and whether each class was confirmed or flagged.
  • Portal account: your email address and sign-in details for our family and tutor portal.
  • Payment details: what you paid and when. Your card is stored by our payment provider, Stripe; we only keep the card type and last 4 digits.
  • Messages: emails and messages you send us.
  • Tutor applicants: name, contact details, education, experience and references.

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you send a form on our website, we receive what you type into it.

3. Children's information

For students under 18, we collect information from the parent or guardian, with their consent, and we communicate about the student with the parent or guardian. Tutors see only what they need to teach: usually the student's first name, grade, subject and learning goals.

4. How we use it

  • To match your child with a tutor, schedule classes and run them.
  • To send you class updates, progress notes, invoices and reminders.
  • To take payment and keep business and tax records.
  • To answer your questions and improve our teaching.
  • To review tutor applications.

We will ask for your consent before using your information for anything else. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

5. Class recordings

We do not record classes unless we tell you beforehand and you agree. If we start recording, we will explain why, who can see the recordings and how long we keep them.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with people and services that help us deliver tutoring, and only what they need:

  • Tutors who teach your child.
  • Video class platform (currently Google Meet) to hold live classes.
  • Email provider (Zoho Mail, with data stored in Canada).
  • Portal and database provider (Supabase), which stores portal accounts, classes and billing records.
  • Automatic email service (Resend), which sends class confirmations and receipts.
  • Payment provider (Stripe), which stores your card and processes weekly payments.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it, or to protect a child's safety.

7. Service providers outside Canada

Some of the people and services we use are located outside Canada, so your information may be collected, used or stored in other countries:

  • Bangladesh and other countries where our tutors live: tutors receive the student's first name, grade, subject, goals and class schedule, so they can teach and write progress notes.
  • United States: our video class platform, automatic email service, payment provider and some technology services may process or store information, to run live classes, send class emails and receipts, and take payments.

Information held outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country, and authorities there may be able to access it. You can ask for written information about our policies for service providers outside Canada, or ask any question about them, by contacting our Privacy Officer (section 11).

8. How we protect it

We use password-protected accounts with two-step login, share information on a need-to-know basis, and ask tutors to keep student information confidential and to use it only for teaching. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the relevant privacy authorities as the law requires.

9. How long we keep it

We keep student and class information while your child is learning with us, and for up to 2 years afterwards in case you return. We keep payment records for as long as tax law requires (usually 6 years). Then we securely delete it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you or your child.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by ending classes. We may then be unable to provide some services.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.

We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell current families about important changes by email.
