Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Print or save a copy

In short: we collect only what we need to teach your child and run our classes. We never sell your information. Parents can always see their child's messages with tutors. Some of our tutors and service providers are outside Canada, and this policy explains where and why. Questions? Email contact@avelonacademy.ca.

1. Who we are

Avelon Academy ("Avelon", "we", "us") provides live online tutoring for students in Grades 1–10. It is operated by Sadman Ahmed, a sole proprietor in Alberta, Canada. We handle personal information in line with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, where it applies, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. What we collect

  • Parent or guardian details: name, email address, phone number and time zone.
  • Student details: first name, grade, subjects, and the student's email if you choose to give them their own login. You may also tell us about learning goals or needs; only tell us what helps us teach.
  • Class information: class schedule and Google Meet links, class length, short progress notes written by tutors, homework, plans for the next class, and whether each class was billed or reported as a problem.
  • Files: worksheets and other files that you, your child or the tutor upload to My Avelon.
  • Messages: emails you send us, and messages sent in My Avelon between your family, your tutor and Avelon (who sent them, when, and what they say).
  • Account and security information: your sign-in email, one-time sign-in codes (stored only in scrambled form) and basic technical logs our providers keep to protect the service, such as sign-in times and IP addresses.
  • Payment details: what you paid and when. Your card is stored by our payment provider, Stripe; we only keep the card type and last 4 digits.
  • Trial requests: what you type into our booking form (name, email, phone, grade and subject).
  • Tutor applicants and tutors: name, contact details, education, experience, references and payment details.

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies or analytics. When you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

3. Consent

  • We collect, use and share personal information with your consent, or where PIPA allows it without consent (for example, to collect a debt or comply with the law).
  • You give consent when you send a trial request, accept our Terms, or give us information for a purpose we've explained. Some consent is implied: for example, if you send us a message, we use your email to reply.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, subject to legal or contract limits. We'll tell you what that means for the service; for example, we can't teach without a student's first name and grade.

4. Children's information

  • Our students are under 18. We collect their information from, and with the consent of, a parent or guardian, and the parent or guardian makes decisions about it.
  • If you give us your child's email, they get their own login to My Avelon to see class times, notes, files and messages. They never see prices or payments.
  • For Grade 6 and up, when the student has their own login, detailed class notes go to the student, and the parent still gets class dates, lengths and every receipt. The parent can ask us for their child's notes at any time.
  • Messages between a student and a tutor are never private from the parent: the parent or guardian and Avelon can read every conversation. Messages that look like they share a phone number, email address, another app or an outside link are flagged automatically so we can review them. This is to keep students safe.
  • Tutors see only what they need to teach: the student's first name, grade, subjects, schedule, class notes, files and messages. Tutors never see your email, phone number, card or payments.
  • In small groups, students see each other's first names and faces in class. We never share contact details with other families.

5. How we use it

  • To match your child with a tutor, schedule classes and run them.
  • To send you sign-in codes, class notices, weekend reports, message alerts, receipts and important updates.
  • To take payment, pay tutors and keep business and tax records.
  • To keep students safe, by reviewing flagged messages and dealing with any concern.
  • To answer your questions, resolve problems and improve our teaching.
  • To review tutor applications.

We will ask for your consent before using your information for any other purpose, including marketing emails. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't use it to train artificial intelligence models.

6. Class recordings

We do not record classes. If we ever want to, we will tell you beforehand, explain why, who can see the recordings and how long we keep them, and record only with your agreement.

7. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with people and services that help us deliver tutoring, and only what they need. Each service provider is bound by contract or its terms to protect it and use it only for our purposes.

  • Tutors who teach your child.
  • Video class platform (Google Meet) to hold live classes.
  • Email provider (Zoho Mail, with data stored in Canada) for our inbox.
  • Portal and database provider (Supabase, with our database in Canada) for accounts, schedules, classes, messages, files and billing records.
  • Automatic email service (Resend), which sends sign-in codes, class emails, weekend reports, message alerts and receipts.
  • Payment provider (Stripe), which stores your card and processes weekly payments.
  • Professional advisers such as an accountant or lawyer, when needed and under confidentiality.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires or allows it, or to protect a child's safety.
  • A buyer of the business, if Avelon is ever sold, only as PIPA allows, and they must use it under this policy.

8. Service providers outside Canada

Some of the people and services we use are outside Canada, so your information may be collected, used, stored or accessed in other countries:

  • Bangladesh and other countries where our tutors live: tutors receive the student's first name, grade, subjects, schedule, class notes, files and messages, so they can teach and write progress notes.
  • United States and other countries: our video class platform, automatic email service, payment provider and some technology services may process or store information, to run live classes, send emails and take payments.

Information held outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country, and courts, law enforcement or other authorities there may be able to access it. You can ask for written information about our policies and practices for service providers outside Canada, or ask any question about how they collect, use, disclose or store personal information for us, by contacting our Privacy Officer (section 12).

9. How we protect it

We use one-time sign-in codes instead of passwords, two-step login on our admin accounts, encrypted connections, database rules so each person sees only their own family's information, and need-to-know access. Tutors agree in writing to keep student information confidential and to use it only for teaching. No system is perfectly secure. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta without unreasonable delay and notify affected people as the law requires.

10. How long we keep it

  • Files uploaded to My Avelon: deleted automatically 3 months after upload.
  • Sign-in codes: expire within 24 hours and are used once.
  • Trial requests that don't become classes: deleted within 12 months.
  • Student, class and message information: while your child is learning with us and for up to 2 years afterwards, in case you return or have a question about a class or charge.
  • Payment and tax records: as long as tax law requires (usually 6 years).

After that, we securely delete or anonymize it. We also keep information longer if the law requires it or if it's needed for an ongoing dispute.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you or your child, and how it has been used and shared.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong. You can also update your name and phone in My Avelon.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by ending classes. We may then be unable to provide some services.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.

Email us to make a request. We may need to confirm your identity, and that you are the student's parent or guardian. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
