Safety and privacy

Built so parents can check everything.

Your child is learning online with someone you haven't met, so every safeguard is one you can verify.

  • 01
    Every session recordedWith your consent. Stored securely, reviewed for quality, and available to you on request.
  • 02
    Contact details never sharedTutors and families never exchange phone numbers, emails or social accounts. Everything runs through Avelon.
  • 03
    Carefully vetted tutorsDegree check, subject test, demo lesson, police clearance and references before anyone teaches.
  • 04
    Run from AlbertaA local founder oversees every tutor. Questions go to a real person, in your time zone.

How it works

Getting started takes a week.

  1. 01

    Free assessment call

    20 minutes to understand the course, upcoming tests and where your child is stuck.

  2. 02

    Matched with a tutor

    We choose a vetted tutor for the subject and set a weekly time that suits after-school hours.

  3. 03

    Two free sessions

    Try it before you commit. If it isn't the right fit, we change the tutor or you walk away.

  4. 04

    Progress you can see

    A summary after every session and a progress report each month.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Who are the tutors?

University-educated tutors from around the world, fluent in English and trained on the Alberta curriculum. Each one passes subject tests and a demo lesson before teaching.

When are sessions?

After school on weekdays, and on weekends, Mountain Time. You get one fixed weekly slot.

Can I watch a recording?

Yes. Ask us and we'll share it. Recordings are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD] and then deleted.

What does my child need?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a quiet spot, and their class notes or worksheet.

What if the tutor isn't a good fit?

Tell us and we'll match you with another tutor at no cost.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by card or pre-authorized debit. No long-term contract.
