FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Who are the tutors?

University-educated tutors, fluent in English, each personally interviewed and vetted by the founder and asked to give a demo lesson before they teach.

How do the 3 trial classes work?

Your child takes 3 classes with their tutor. If it isn't the right fit, you pay nothing. If you continue, the first class stays free and classes 2 and 3 go on your first weekly bill.

Do you teach young children?

Yes, from Grade 1, one-on-one with the same patient tutor each week.

One-on-one or group?

Both. One-on-one gives your child a dedicated tutor. Small groups have 2–3 students in the same grade and subject. Ask for a group in My Avelon, your account on our site, and we'll match your child if we can.

When are classes?

After school on weekdays and on weekends, in your local time zone. You get a fixed weekly time.

What if my child misses a class?

Tell us at least 24 hours before and we'll reschedule at no charge.

Is there homework?

Usually a little practice after each class, so new skills stick. We can also help with school homework.

What does my child need?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a quiet spot, and their class notes or worksheet.

What if the tutor isn't a good fit?

Tell us and we'll match you with another tutor.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any time. There's no contract and no hidden fees: you only pay for classes your child has taken.

How do I pay?

Add a card in My Avelon. After each class, you (or your child) confirm it with one tap. Every Monday we charge the card for the previous week's confirmed classes and email you a receipt.
