1. About these terms

These terms are an agreement between you and Avelon Academy ("Avelon", "we", "us"), a tutoring business based in Alberta, Canada. By booking a class, you agree to them. If the student is under 18, the parent or guardian who books accepts these terms on the student's behalf.

2. Our services

We provide live online tutoring for Grades 1–10 in Math, Science, English and Bangla, one-on-one or in small groups. Classes are held by video call. Grades 1–2 classes are 30 minutes; other classes are 60 minutes unless we agree otherwise.

3. Trial classes

  • New students start with 3 trial classes with their tutor.
  • If you decide not to continue after the trial, you pay nothing for the 3 classes.
  • If you continue, class 1 is free and classes 2 and 3 are charged at your plan's rate on your first weekly bill.
  • To continue after the trial, you need to add a payment card in My Avelon, your account on our website.
  • The trial is offered once per student.

4. Fees, class records and payment

  • Your hourly price is shown in My Avelon, in Canadian dollars, before you save a card. You pay only for classes your child takes.
  • After each class, the tutor logs it in My Avelon (date, length and notes) and we email you. You don't need to confirm anything.
  • Classes are charged at the length booked for that class, even if it runs over.
  • If something isn't right with a class (for example, it didn't happen or was shorter), use "Report a problem" in the email or in My Avelon within 48 hours of the class being logged. A class you report is not charged until we have looked into it with you.
  • If no problem is reported within 48 hours, the class is billed.
  • Every Monday, we charge the card saved in My Avelon for classes that have passed the 48-hour window and haven't been charged yet, and email you a receipt. By saving a card, you authorize Avelon Academy to make these weekly charges until you remove the card or stop classes.
  • Card details are stored securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Any discounts (for example, for siblings) are shown in My Avelon.
  • We may change our prices with at least 30 days' notice by email. Classes already taken are charged at the price that applied at the time.
  • If a payment fails, we will email you and try again on the next billing day. We may pause classes until an unpaid amount is settled.
  • If you think a charge is wrong, email us within 14 days of the charge and we will review it and refund any mistake.

5. Scheduling, cancellations and missed classes

  • You can cancel or reschedule a class at no charge if you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • With less than 24 hours' notice, or if the student doesn't attend, the class counts as taken.
  • If the student joins late, the class still ends at its scheduled time.
  • If we or the tutor cancel or miss a class, you are not charged and we will offer another time.

6. Pausing and stopping

There is no contract. You can pause or stop classes at any time by emailing us or removing your card in My Avelon. You pay only for classes already taken (and any classes cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice).

7. Online classes

  • The student needs a laptop or tablet with a camera, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to learn.
  • For Grades 1–2, a parent or guardian should be nearby during class.
  • Please arrange all scheduling, changes and payments through Avelon, not directly with the tutor.
  • You can message your tutor in My Avelon. Each conversation can be read by the parent or guardian, the student (if they have their own login), the tutor and Avelon. Please keep messages about classes and don't share phone numbers, personal emails or other apps. We review messages that appear to share contact details, to keep students safe.
  • If a technical problem on our side stops a class, we will reschedule it.

8. Respect and safety

We expect respectful behaviour from students, families and tutors. We may pause or end services if a student or family is abusive, harasses a tutor, or repeatedly breaks these terms. In that case you pay only for classes already taken. If we have any concern about a child's safety, we may contact the parent or guardian or the appropriate authorities.

Most problems are easy to fix when we hear about them early, so we ask that we try to sort things out together first.
