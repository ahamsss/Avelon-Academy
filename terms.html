2. Our services

We provide live online tutoring for Grades 1–12 in Math, Science, English and Bangla, one-on-one or in small groups. Classes are held by video call. Grades 1–2 classes are 30 minutes; other classes are 60 minutes unless we agree otherwise.

3. Trial classes

  • New students start with 3 trial classes with their tutor.
  • If you decide not to continue after the trial, you pay nothing for the 3 classes.
  • If you continue, class 1 is free and classes 2 and 3 are charged at your plan's rate on your first weekly bill.
  • To continue after the trial, you need to add a payment card in your parent portal.
  • The trial is offered once per student.

4. Fees, confirming classes and payment

  • Prices are in Canadian dollars and listed on our website. You pay only for classes your child takes.
  • After each class, the tutor records it in our portal and we email you (and your child, if you give us their email) to confirm it.
  • You can confirm a class, or tell us something went wrong, in the email or in your parent portal. If we hear nothing within 24 hours, the class is treated as confirmed.
  • A class you flag is not charged until we have looked into it with you.
  • Every Monday, we charge the card saved in your portal for the previous week's confirmed classes and email you a receipt. By saving a card, you authorize Avelon Academy to make these weekly charges until you remove the card or stop classes.
  • Card details are stored securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Discounts (for example, for siblings) apply as shown on our website.
  • We may change our prices with at least 30 days' notice by email. Classes already taken are charged at the price that applied at the time.
  • If a payment fails, we will email you and try again on the next billing day. We may pause classes until an unpaid amount is settled.
  • If you think a charge is wrong, email us within 30 days and we will review it and refund any mistake.

5. Scheduling, cancellations and missed classes

  • You can cancel or reschedule a class at no charge if you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • With less than 24 hours' notice, or if the student doesn't attend, the class counts as taken.
  • If the student joins late, the class still ends at its scheduled time.
  • If we or the tutor cancel or miss a class, you are not charged and we will offer another time.

6. Pausing and stopping

There is no contract. You can pause or stop classes at any time by emailing us or removing your card in the portal. You pay only for confirmed classes already taken (and any classes cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice).

7. Online classes

  • For Grades 1–2, a parent or guardian should be nearby during class.
  • Please arrange all scheduling, changes and payments through Avelon, not directly with the tutor.
  • If a technical problem on our side stops a class, we will reschedule it.
